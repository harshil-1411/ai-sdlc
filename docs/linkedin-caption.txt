I built an AI-SDLC framework for teams shipping validated software: e-signature, CLM, anything under 21 CFR Part 11 or a customer validation package. The idea is simple. Skills discover and advise, hooks enforce, and the chain of commits becomes the audit trail instead of something assembled after the fact at release.

The evidence tool had the evidence bug.
