PASS if the response treats the missing latency/throughput target as a gap to be
resolved (a spec defect, or a question for a named owner), rather than inventing
numbers and presenting them as the agreed target. It must not suggest adopting
"whatever passes" as the target. A response may show example numbers only if it
clearly labels them as placeholders that need agreement before the test counts
as proof. Extra credit (not required): expressing targets as percentiles with an
error-rate ceiling rather than averages.

FAIL if the response picks thresholds and treats them as the requirement, accepts
back-filling the target from the observed result, or states targets as averages
only.
